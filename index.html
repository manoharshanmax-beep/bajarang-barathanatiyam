OUR STORY

Our Journey
Since 2004

Bajrang Narthanalaya was established in 2004 and has been successfully serving the field of Bharatanatyam ever since, with the divine blessings of Shenbagavalli Amman Udanurai Bhuvananatha Swami.

Every year, Bajrang Narthanalaya conducts various traditional and cultural programmes, including Annual Day celebrations, Natyanjali, Salangai Poojai, and Arangetram.

Students are systematically trained in Bharatanatyam and guided towards pursuing Certificate Courses, Bachelor's Degrees, and Master's Degrees in Bharatanatyam.
